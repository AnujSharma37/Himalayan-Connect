Digital Promotion

Simple social media tips to reach more tourists.

📸

Post clear photos

Show your rooms, views and products in daylight. Post at least twice a week.

💬

Use WhatsApp Business

Add your prices and a greeting message so guests can book quickly.

#️⃣

Add hashtags

Try #VisitHimalayas, #Homestay and your village name on every post.

🎬

Share short videos

A 15 second clip of sunrise, cooking or weaving gets noticed fast.

⭐

Ask for reviews

Ask happy guests to rate you on Google Maps and share a photo.

📍

Pin your location

List your business on Google Maps so visitors can find the way.

Sample post

“Wake up to snow peaks and fresh mountain tea. Rooms available this season! Message us on WhatsApp to book. #VisitHimalayas #Homestay”
